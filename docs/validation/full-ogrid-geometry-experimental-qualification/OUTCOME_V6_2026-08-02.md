## What failed, and why

The three-grid convergence gate.

| Level | Cells | h (m) | Kinematic pressure drop |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| coarse | 2,496 | 2.2097e-3 | 0.019980739 |
| medium | 19,968 | 1.1049e-3 | 0.023462337 |
| fine | 159,744 | 5.5243e-4 | 0.023063852 |

- coarse to medium: **+3.4816e-3**
- medium to fine: **-3.98485e-4**

The sequence is **not monotone**: it overshoots at medium and returns at fine.
Richardson extrapolation requires a monotone sequence, so `verification.py`
refuses to compute an observed order or a fine-grid GCI at all. Both are
therefore `null` rather than large, and `mathematicallyValid` is false.

That refusal is the frozen guard behaving correctly. A GCI computed from a
non-monotone sequence would be meaningless, and the contract explicitly sets
`mathematicallyInvalidGciPasses: false`.

## What may not be concluded

- This is **not** a statement that the full O-grid geometry is wrong. Every
  per-level gate passed, including exact cell identity at 159,744 cells.
- The change in the quantity of interest shrinks by a factor of about 8.7 from
  the first refinement to the second, which is *consistent with* the coarse
  level lying outside the asymptotic range. That is a **hypothesis, not a
  result**. It may not be asserted, and it may not be tested by re-running this
  contract with a different grid family.
- Testing it requires a **new prospectively declared contract** with a new
  campaign id and a fresh immutable directory, declaring the grid family before
  execution. Selecting a grid family after seeing this sequence would be exactly
  the post-hoc threshold shopping the project forbids.
